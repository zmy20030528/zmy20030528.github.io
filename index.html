About

I am a master’s student in Computer Technology at Wuhan University of Technology (2025–present). I studied Artificial Intelligence at Wuhan University of Technology from 2020 to 2024.

My research interests include face super-resolution and image compression, with a focus on high-quality visual reconstruction and efficient representation.
